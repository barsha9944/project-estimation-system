## Additional Quality Assurance Practices

- Prior to launch, our dedicated team of Project Managers, Developers, and Creative Professionals ensures that every application undergoes a rigorous quality control and testing process. This process also ensures that the customer has access to analytics and performance visibility for the application.

- **Proofing Process** — We understand the importance of application content in engaging target audiences and effectively communicating brand identity. Our internal proofing process ensures that all content and design elements are carefully reviewed by multiple reviewers before development and deployment.

- **Cross-Browser Testing** — Cross-browser testing is an essential part of our quality assurance practice, especially for responsive applications. Testing is conducted across multiple devices and browsers including desktops, tablets, smartphones, and various browser versions to ensure consistent behavior and user experience.

- Testing activities are typically completed using modern browsers including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge.

- Responsive application designs are optimized for multiple screen resolutions including:
  - 320px – 1023px
  - 1024px and above

- Ensure stable and scalable application delivery
- Minimize production defects

- Validate cross-platform compatibility
