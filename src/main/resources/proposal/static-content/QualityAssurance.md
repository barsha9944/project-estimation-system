BEAS creates and implements a quality control process specifically designed for customers’ development projects with focus on the following objectives:

- Reduction of unanticipated problems
- Streamlined execution
- Rapid resolution of issues and prevention of recurring defects
- Optimal communication among work teams and individual staff
- Frequent checks against overall project quality standards
- Formal review processes throughout the SDLC

## Project Artifacts Under Review Control

Almost all artifacts produced during the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) are subject to formal reviews.

The following project artifacts are typically reviewed and controlled:

- Project Management Plan (PMP) document
- Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document
- Design Document (DD)
- Test Plan document and Test Cases
- Source Code
- Test Results
- Testing Reports

## Testing Activities

The following testing practices are performed during software development projects:

- Unit Testing
- Unit / Integration Testing
- System Testing
- Acceptance Testing (UAT)

## Quality Governance

- Continuous quality monitoring is maintained throughout the project lifecycle.
- Defect tracking and reporting mechanisms are established for transparency.
- Testing outcomes and quality metrics are periodically reviewed with stakeholders.
- UAT support is provided to ensure successful business validation and signoff.
